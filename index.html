The Overture

Inspired by the profound complexity of the Baroque period and the enduring genius of J.S. Bach, the Meghalaya Philharmonic Society is a modern vessel for classical excellence. We exist to harmonize indigenous local talents with universal musical traditions, orchestrating a cultural renaissance across Meghalaya and beyond.

Our Movements

I.

Virtuosity

Promoting indigenous local talents in music and instituting prestigious awards for outstanding achievements.

II.

Harmony

Seeking collaboration with leading music studios and schools across the country, including Bollywood and international stages.

III.

Conservatory

Setting up modern schools for imparting training to talented artists, bridging classical theory with contemporary execution.

IV.

Performance

Advancing public education and appreciation of music through grand public concerts and recitals.

V.

Symphony

Providing premier orchestral and musical entertainment in Meghalaya and creating a thriving musical ecosystem.

VI.

Mastery

Organizing and sponsoring intensive training programmes, workshops, conferences, and academic seminars.
