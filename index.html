Our Aims & Objectives

Promote Local Talent

Promoting indigenous local talents in music and instituting awards for outstanding achievements.

Global Collaboration

Seeking collaboration with leading music studios and schools across the country, including Bollywood and international fame.

Music Education

Setting up schools for imparting training to talented artists and advancing public education in the art of music.

Public Concerts

Presenting public concerts, recitals, and providing orchestral entertainment in Meghalaya.

Skill Development

Organizing and sponsoring training programmes, workshops, conferences, and seminars.
